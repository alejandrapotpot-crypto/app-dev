# My Favorite Movie

## About the Movie

My favorite movie is **Doctor Strange**. It is a superhero movie about Dr. Stephen Strange, a talented surgeon who gets into a car accident and damages his hands. He travels to find a cure and discovers the world of magic. He later becomes a powerful sorcerer who protects the world from dangerous enemies.

---

### Why I Like It

I like this movie because:

- The story is interesting and unique.
- The magical effects are amazing.
- The characters are entertaining.
- The action scenes are exciting.
- It has a good combination of magic, action, and adventure.

### My Favorite Characters

1. Doctor Strange
2. Wong
3. Christine Palmer
4. The Ancient One

### My Rating

**9/10**

> Doctor Strange is one of my favorite superhero movies because it has amazing visual effects, exciting action scenes, and an interesting story.
